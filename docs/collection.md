# Continuous collection

Normal app startup starts a credential-free background worker unless collection was explicitly stopped. The worker holds a process lock for its data root. Closing the browser or restarting the web listener does not stop an existing worker. Use the [isolated walkthrough](../README.md#open-the-app) for offline work.

Open **Captures and source health** for source status and **Stop collection** / **Start collection**. Stop persists in `collector/paused` across browser, listener, and worker restarts. An in-flight request can finish and its receipt may be preserved; subsequent requests wait for Start. The same controls are available from the checkout root. Pass `--data /path/to/root` if your listener uses a non-default root.

```sh
uv run --locked python -m weather_edge.collector status
uv run --locked python -m weather_edge.collector stop
uv run --locked python -m weather_edge.collector start
```

The listener also owns a separate [paper reconciler](audit-paper-ledger-verification.md). It starts even under `--no-collect` and can advance paper orders from retained evidence while collection is stopped. It does not request missing upstream evidence. The [quiet backup procedure](archive-recovery.md#take-check-and-restore-a-backup) accounts for both writers.

## Station scope and source jobs

The core six are **KNYC, KMIA, KLAX, KPHL, KMDW and KSFO**. The combined Aviation Weather Center query also covers KAUS, KATL, KBOS, KDCA, KDEN, KDFW, KEWR, KLAS, KMSY, KORD, KSEA, KTTN, KBUF, KCVG, KPDX and KPVD (22 total). Station and instrument coverage vary by job. See the [worker schedule](../weather_edge/collector.py) and [venue sampler](../weather_edge/venue_sources.py) for current limits and scope.

| Nominal check interval | Jobs and scope |
| --- | --- |
| 15 seconds | **Polymarket US daily highs:** up to four station events from recent retained listings. The selection favors Central Park, Miami and Los Angeles, picks one event per station, and can roll to the next unclosed event before rediscovery. Each round publishes an indexed capture. |
| 15 seconds | **Polymarket US lows and rain:** up to 16 exact instruments from Central Park and Midway priority events, including up to two still-open prior-day rain targets when there is room. This lane retains individual native receipts and batch publication in the market archive. |
| 15 seconds | **Kalshi public books:** up to 72 instruments by default in one batch, choosing open groups near the current UTC date. Priority covers the six core high series, Central Park and Chicago low series, selected daily rain stations and rotating hourly index cities, then other preferred series as capacity allows. |
| 90 seconds | Core-six NWS observations, combined 22-station AWC METAR, and a separate Kalshi weather index feed. |
| 5 minutes | Polymarket US high discovery, both venues' open catalog refresh, and high-capture payout checks. The catalog refresh includes its own broader series and settlement work. |
| 10 minutes | Core-six NOAA NBM hourly and short-range guidance; seven-station extended NBM outlook (KCVG, KNYC, KMDW, KORD, KDEN, KPDX, KBUF); NDBC marine observations and MRMS radar. The Iowa State MADIS HFMETAR task checks its timer each minute and normally requests the core six at most every 10 minutes, subject to backoff. |
| 15 minutes | Core-six NWS Daily Climate Reports and a separate NOAA MADIS five-minute airport observation job. |
| 20 minutes | GFS temperature, clouds and wind, GFS precipitation, GFS Wave and wave components, HRRR station and intraday guidance, and local market search maintenance. |

These are scheduler intervals, not guarantees of new data or publication. A running job is not launched again until it finishes. Some jobs wait for new upstream cycles or honor retries. **Last attempt**, **last successful update** and **source time** answer different questions.

## Diagnose a missing price or paper recheck

1. In **Captures and source health**, open the relevant venue lane and its last completed batch. A current open instrument can be outside that batch's priority slots. Absence from the selected targets is a sampling gap; an older capture without batch telemetry cannot establish selection. **Unrequested** means a selected target had no request. **Failed**, **missing or invalid**, and **empty depth** distinguish a request error, an omitted/unusable response, and a valid response with no usable size.
2. Open the exact venue, instrument and side from the health row or **Prices**. A listing offer can show a price without size, and a usable displayed ladder can be cached or stale. **Fetch public book** is an explicit public request through the collector for an open instrument; it doesn't change the automatic batch or guarantee eligibility.
3. In **Book evidence**, compare the exchange image time (when the venue says the ladder was formed), the HTTP receipt (when it arrived locally), and first app availability (when the completed capture or batch made it usable). A recent HTTP receipt can carry an old exchange image. An image can arrive before the end of a recheck window but be published after it. Check the exact listing, clock, fee, model, and recheck blockers in the ticket. See the [retained Miami and Kalshi examples](capture.md#paper-orders-and-recheck-clocks).

Weather values and telemetry live in `weather.sqlite3`; identical values keep their first known receipt. The high capture tape is in `captures/` with its own index. Native book batches, venue receipts and catalog history live in `venues.sqlite3`. A damaged capture does not make other sessions unreadable. See [backup and failure diagnosis](archive-recovery.md) and the [verified source-to-ticket examples](audit-collector-verification.md).

## Initial live evidence · November 15, 2026

The first bounded capture ran November 15, 2026 at 06:56:41–06:57:39Z. Eight rounds returned 192 displayed ladder images across Central Park, Miami, Midway and Los Angeles. The session also saved one NBM date-specific forecast at each station and thirteen 1° GFS temperature and cloud frames apiece. Los Angeles did not yet have same-day readings for the Nov. 15 climate window because that window began at 08:00Z.

The continuous worker first refreshed the catalog at 07:06:53Z. It found 24 weather events, including 14 daily highs, four daily lows, three rain events, two global annual temperature rankings and a San Jose monthly temperature contract. It sampled daily highs for KNYC, KMIA, KLAX and KPHL. It received six-station NOAA 06Z forecasts and added completed climate reports for November 11–14. The KNYC report for Nov. 14, published at 06:17Z on Nov. 15, gives an official high of 46°F, low of 38°F and 0.00 inches of precipitation. [NWS CLI product](https://api.weather.gov/products/3b64bade-d3e9-4c4d-a694-7e487ef26f30), [weather listings](https://gateway.polymarket.us/v2/tags/weather/events?limit=50).

The Iowa State retry succeeded at 07:17:31Z and saved 88 five-minute records for five stations. The Miami 06:50Z record measured 64.4°F with a 48.2°F dew point, adding a time absent from its routine source records. It arrived roughly 28 minutes after observation in this sample. The feed improved density, with no timing improvement established. The sample had no qualifying Central Park HFMETAR. Browse **Latest capture**, then select Miami for the supplementary observation series. [IEM HFMETAR provider](https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/cgi-bin/request/asos.py?help).

A live Central Park 49–50°F book request at 07:16:45Z returned a Cloudflare cache hit with `Age: 17`, `Cache-Control: public, max-age=30`, HTTP Last-Modified 07:16:31Z, and an exchange transaction timestamp of 07:16:09.784Z. The request used the collector's normal `Cache-Control: no-cache`. That request directive expresses a client preference, and the public book endpoint provides no freshness guarantee. New receipts retain the relevant cache headers. Receipts with positive Age remain visible for inspection. The primary paper trial requires fresh evidence for a reaction recheck. [Public book schema](https://docs.polymarket.us/api-reference/markets/get-market-book), [HTTP caching specification](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9111.html#section-5.2.1).

## Source and contract research

- **MET Norway Locationforecast Compact.** A live Central Park point request returned 88 forecast periods, with an update time of 05:19:55Z and a first valid point at 06:00Z. It gave 4.8°C, 29.7% cloud, 3.1 m/s wind and zero mm next-hour precipitation. MET Norway documents ECMWF global guidance, distinct from NOAA GFS, with interpolated locations and hourly or six-hourly valid values. The API returned Last-Modified and Expires. Its terms require a human in authority to accept them for service use. The ongoing collection uses the public NOAA and Iowa State sources. [MET Norway forecast data model](https://docs.api.met.no/doc/locationforecast/datamodel.html), [terms](https://docs.api.met.no/doc/TermsOfService).
- **Open-Meteo Single Runs.** A named GFS run initialized November 5 at 12:00Z returned a Central Park grid point at 40.826553°N, 73.94531°W. The 12:00Z values were 15.7°C with missing cloud cover. The 13:00Z forecast was 16.8°C, 100% cloud and 10.5 km/h wind. The GFS model is the same NOAA forecast family as the globe. The free service allows noncommercial use. It provides initialization and valid times. Direct NOAA data also retains the S3 publication timestamp used by the replay. [Single Runs](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/single-runs-api), [GFS resolution and cadence](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/gfs-api), [free API terms](https://open-meteo.com/en/pricing).
- **Station archives and Python.** Iowa State's representative five-minute request returned KMIA and KLAX raw HFMETAR observations between routine reports, with blank derived temperature columns and ASOS tenths groups in the raw report. The collector keeps those records as a supplementary trace when available. The first scheduled request returned HTTP 429 while other feeds continued. The historical archive uses NOAA MADIS receipts and the delayed one-minute ASOS dataset where available. Meteostat hourly bulk data can include model substitutes and does not add the required original local receipt clocks. Herbie and xarray can select indexed GRIB records. The app's existing ecCodes reader already validates the exact NOAA fields and time metadata, so collection reuses it. [IEM report parameters](https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/cgi-bin/request/asos.py?help), [NOAA MADIS METAR](https://madis.ncep.noaa.gov/madis_metar.shtml), [NCEI ASOS records](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/land-based-station/automated-surface-weather-observing-systems), [Meteostat bulk hourly](https://dev.meteostat.net/data/bulk/hourly.html), [Herbie](https://herbie.readthedocs.io/en/stable/).
- **Daily lows are the closest next model.** The actual Nov. 15 New York contract's 34–35°F bucket references KNYC's NWS Daily Climate Report and the whole-degree minimum within the fixed local standard day. The corresponding Midway Nov. 15 listing uses KMDW. The app already archives both stations' official minima, and a follow-on study needs as-issued minimum guidance and validation by climate day. The daily rain family has a different report selection rule. The Nov. 15 New York rain market uses KNYC's CLI liquid-equivalent total of at least 0.01 inches. A trace or missing total resolves No under its terms. It uses the final eligible complete report through 2 p.m. ET the next day, with a later-report fallback. That makes exact products worth collecting even where the current weather model is for daily highs. [New York low listing](https://gateway.polymarket.us/v1/events/slug/temp-nyclow-2026-11-15), [Midway low listing](https://gateway.polymarket.us/v1/events/slug/temp-mdwlow-2026-11-15), [daily rain listing](https://gateway.polymarket.us/v1/events/slug/rain-daily-2026-11-15), [exchange temperature filing](https://www.cftc.gov/IndustryOversight/IndustryFilings/TradingOrganizationProducts/60333).
- **Other families.** The active San Jose December listing uses the NWS SJC report and a strictly-below-40°F threshold on any day in December 2026. The annual ranking listings use the first published NASA GISS Land-Ocean Temperature Index annual figure, including tied ranks. This catalog contained no distinct snow or named-storm listings. [San Jose listing](https://gateway.polymarket.us/v1/events/slug/temp-sjc-dec), [2026 annual ranking](https://gateway.polymarket.us/v1/events/slug/yr-httst-rnk-glbl-2026), [NASA GISS global index](https://data.giss.nasa.gov/gistemp/).
