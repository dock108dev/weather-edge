It models an ordinary low-volume Tier 1 taker outside onboarding, without rebates, promotions, October volume relief or account-specific overrides. In paper, the reviewed decision clock plays that role: a delayed book uses its own prices and the earlier locked coefficient. For 100 contracts at 50¢ just after the boundary, the same ladder budgets $1.74 from an earlier locked signal or $2.38 from a new signal at the boundary. - [Kalshi July 7 general schedule](https://kalshi.com/docs/kalshi-fee-schedule.pdf) documents the `0.07 × multiplier` quadratic base. The [rounding docs](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/fee_rounding) distinguish direct-member precision from non-direct-member cent balances. This indicative estimate uses whole lots, rounds modeled fees up to micros at each used price level, then aligns the all-in debit up to cents. Public levels can aggregate multiple orders; actual matching and clearing can vary.
