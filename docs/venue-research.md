If a Research record opens without a frozen download, check whether its referenced file was retained. ## Matched historical price benchmark protocol

The fixed analysis target is the observed whole-degree high or low reported for the same NWS climate station and local standard day. This makes the cases a common weather benchmark even when the native contracts use different payout criteria. Both native payouts are retained separately and disagreements stay in the sample. The primary question is whether either venue's coherent probability proxy has a lower Brier score on the same held-out cases. A simple average checks whether combining two proxies helps. An uncalibrated as-issued NOAA distribution is included on the subset where the required issue, valid day, clock and spread are known.

Use October 1–31, 2026 to fit a logistic combiner on the two market probabilities. Use November 3–14 for the untouched comparison. The intervening dates give earlier reports time to publish. A label released after the first test decision cannot be used to fit the combiner. The decision clocks are 03:05, 09:05, 13:05, 17:05 and 21:05 local standard time. These are five of the established weather study clocks with five minutes added for publication order. Pair actual native thresholds on the exact station and date. Cases with duplicate contracts for the same venue and threshold are excluded when they cannot be uniquely paired.

Use Kalshi bid and ask closing values at the exact hourly candle end. Use the Polymarket US long display value as the ask proxy and one minus the short display as the bid proxy at that minute, with a two-minute lookback for the nearest stored point. Require both sides strictly inside the unit interval and a spread between zero and $0.30. The midpoint is a probability proxy. A fixed 10-cent spread sensitivity and the width distribution show how this eligibility choice affects coverage. Candle and display records support prediction tests and carry no executable size. The timestamps represent provider series clocks and do not establish historical time of local receipt. [Kalshi candle schema](https://docs.kalshi.com/api-reference/market/get-market-candlesticks) · [Polymarket US display price schema](https://docs.polymarket.us/api-reference/price-history/get-price-history)

Use equal weight by calendar date in the Brier comparison. Report independent calendar dates and station-days alongside bucket comparisons, and use a paired bootstrap over dates for descriptive uncertainty. Compare the historically served data on the same cases at each time and separately report tail support. This analysis remains complete when the predeclared test dates have been scored or an absent source blocks a case. Provider coverage and number of independent dates determine the interpretation.
