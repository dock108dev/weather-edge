## What delayed the browser

On the first overlapping baseline load, the exact ticket took 43.40 s on the server and 44.27 s to become usable. The server opened 13,023 indexed high captures. The high capture read and merge held its lock for 14.38 s, including 9.14 s in the indexed read. The same request assembled six native low events from 126,145 book rows in a separate 27.51 s phase. It spent 19.49 s in the book-query calls, including the SQL reads and JSON decodes, and another 8.02 s on listing history and event assembly. It then built the one selected ticket in 0.26 s. The accompanying compact Weather session waited about 15 s on the high capture lock and independently repeated the six native low joins. Its JSON serialization, compression, and send together took less than 0.06 s.

On the isolated board phase probe, catalog selection took 0.02 s. The two-venue row phase took 1.66–1.67 s. Listing reconstruction accounted for 1.61–1.62 s of that time and inference on the recent board took less than 1 ms in the HTTP traces. Under overlap the row phase grew to 4.49 s on the cold baseline board and 5.95 s on its warm repeat. Sending the full board was about 0.01–0.02 s. The separate funnel summary spent about 0.01 s on funnel counts after constructing the same rows. - A compact high-temperature timeline still needs the station menu, first and latest clocks, and the original high tape. The low-event summary path takes the clock bounds from the native market/time index and uses them for the same listing schedule and session bounds. - Every board read still queries listing receipt metadata at its decision cutoff. It can reuse a successful extraction for the same compressed body, reply shape, and requested instrument set. Listing identity checks, simultaneous receipt conflicts, compacted revisions, fees, prices, and paper decisions are recomputed for the request. A decompression failure is retried on the next read. ## Remaining latency

A cold compact session still loads and merges the entire high capture tape after the directory revision changes. The candidate cold overlapping ticket spent 6.31 s in the indexed read and another 5.75 s assembling that tape. Loading a selected native low timeline still requires actual ladder history. Its isolated bound session returned in 7.88 s in the candidate sample. A focused follow-up could measure one cold high merge after a single publication with the SQLite page cache warm, and count how much of the retained timeline each selected high event actually needs. The lazy `/api/paper` read also still assembles the full native ladder history when the paper panel enters view. Its cost may contribute to the observed reload variance. A per-request trace of that route during one Forecast ahead reload would distinguish its work from the session, ticket and decision. These are residual performance cases after the demonstrated normal-flow improvement.
